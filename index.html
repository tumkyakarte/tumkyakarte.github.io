Episode ideas

Payments app

"Galat UPI pe ₹5,000 chale gaye"

A wrong transfer, then a "helpful" caller asking for an OTP. In Part 2 the lead hangs up and raises a dispute in your app in a few taps.

Home camera

"Raat 3 baje darwaze pe koi"

A voice outside claims to be a relative. Before anyone opens the door, the doorbell camera shows who it really is.

Edtech

"Paper leak ka message aaya"

A forwarded "leaked paper" the night before the exam. The choice: trust the message, or report it and use the app's mock tests.

Brand safe

  • Original animated stories. No gore, no real people, no real crimes.
  • No real phone numbers, UPI IDs or number plates on screen, only placeholders.
  • Every paid post carries Instagram's "Paid partnership" label and #ad, as ASCI requires.
  • About a quarter of followers are 13–17, so every story stays family-safe.
  • We don't work with betting, real-money gaming, unregistered loan apps or crypto schemes.
